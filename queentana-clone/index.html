Common questions

FAQs

Who does Queentana Fresh supply?

We supply retailers, wholesalers, foodservice distributors, and hospitality businesses across the UK and EU. Whether you’re a local greengrocer or a large catering operator, we tailor supply solutions to match your needs.

Where are you based?

Our head office is in London, with distribution networks across major UK and European cities.

Do you deliver directly to markets and wholesalers?

Yes. We supply wholesalers, catering businesses, major depots, produce markets and independent retailers. Orders are packed and dispatched for next-day delivery where available.

What products do you specialise in?

We specialise in core vegetables, onions, root crops, exotic vegetables, tropical fruits, citrus, chillies, ginger, and garlic. Our strength lies in sweet potatoes, fine beans, chillies, and other specialty produce sourced globally.

How can I open a trade account?

Simply complete our online application form with your company, banking, and reference details. Once submitted, our credit team will review your request and confirm approval within 1–2 business days.

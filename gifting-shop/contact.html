FAQ

How long does shipping take?

Most gifts ship in 1–2 days. Personalized items need 2–4 extra days. You can pick an occasion delivery date at checkout.

What is your return policy?

Non-personalized items may be returned within 7 days if unused and in original packaging. Personalized and perishable gifts are final sale.

How long does personalization take?

Engraving and monograms typically add 2–4 business days. Corporate logo runs are quoted per order.

Do you offer COD?

Yes, cash on delivery is available on eligible pin codes for orders under ₹10,000.

Can I track my order?

Yes — use Order tracking with your order ID from the confirmation email.

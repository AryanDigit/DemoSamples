About us

Gifts with presence.

Liora began with a simple belief: the right gift should feel personal before it’s unwrapped.

Our story

From small studio to occasion-ready curation

We started by wrapping presents for friends who never knew what to send. Today Liora sources candles, jewelry, hampers, and keepsakes from makers who share our standards for finish, scent, and packaging.

Every product is photographed in consistent light, checked for gifting readiness, and tagged by occasion and recipient so browsing feels calm — not cluttered.

Mission

Make thoughtful gifting effortless: personalization that arrives on time, packaging that feels premium, and support that answers quickly on WhatsApp or email.

Craft & sourcing

We partner with small ateliers and vetted brands. Materials are chosen for longevity where it matters — leather, metals, ceramics — and for delight where it should — chocolate, scent, and wrap.
